Teeth whitening

Coffee, tea, and years of wear leave stains that home kits rarely lift evenly. In-clinic whitening uses controlled gels so enamel stays protected while shade improves in a single, supervised session.

We start with a shade check, agree on a realistic target, then guide you through aftercare so the result stays even, not blotchy a week later.

  • Shade assessment before treatment
  • Enamel-safe professional whitening gels
  • Clear home-care instructions

Clear aligners

Crowding and small gaps can be corrected without metal braces. A digital scan builds a step-by-step plan you can preview before the first tray arrives.

Trays are removable for meals and brushing, so daily life stays simple. We check progress at comfortable intervals and adjust the series if your bite needs a finer finish.

  • Digital scan and treatment preview
  • Series of precisely fitted trays
  • Progress checks along the way
Book a consultation

Veneers

Chips, gaps, and uneven color can make a smile feel unfinished. Thin ceramic veneers refine shape and shade so the result looks like your teeth, only calmer and more even.

We plan proportions against your face, not a stock template. Where possible we keep preparation light, then bond ceramics chosen for strength and stain resistance.

  • Mock-up and shade matching
  • Conservative preparation where possible
  • Durable, stain-resistant ceramics
Book a consultation

Dental implants

A missing tooth affects chewing, speech, and the teeth around it. An implant places a titanium foundation in the bone, then a custom crown that feels secure when you eat and speak.

Planning covers bone, bite, and neighboring teeth so the restoration lasts. Healing guidance and a matched crown finish the process.

  • Clinical and imaging review
  • Precise placement and healing guidance
  • Crown matched to neighboring teeth

Preventive care

Most problems are quieter, and cheaper, when caught early. Regular exams and cleanings keep gums and enamel healthy and give us a clear baseline for your smile over time.

Visits stay calm and practical: a full check, a professional clean, and advice you can actually follow at home.

  • Comprehensive oral examination
  • Professional cleaning and polish
  • Personal home-care advice

Restorative dentistry

Decay, chips, and worn edges take comfort and confidence with them. Tooth-colored fillings and crowns rebuild strength so you can chew normally again.

Materials and shade are chosen to blend with your natural teeth. We repair what is needed and leave healthy structure alone.

  • Tooth-colored restorations
  • Crowns for strength and longevity
  • Repair of chips and worn edges
